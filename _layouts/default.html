02 — Subleases · on iOS

Sublet to a verified student,
on a real agreement.

Summer internship, study abroad, early graduation — a sublease always comes down to trust and paperwork. The app handles both.

  • ·

    AI-drafted sublease agreement. Describe your terms in plain words; the app writes a clean, state-aware agreement in about a minute.

  • ·

    Signed in the app. Both sides e-sign on their phone — no printing, no PDFs passed around over text.

  • ·

    University verification. Students confirm their campus with a .edu email or student ID, so you know who you are renting to.

  • ·

    Built-in chat. Ask about the room, agree on dates, keep the whole thread in one place.
