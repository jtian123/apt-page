# Apateu text alerts

Apateu LLC runs the Apateu iOS app, where college students find and post sublets. This page shows how our text messages work and how people sign up for them.

## How people sign up

To post a listing or send a like, a user confirms their US mobile number in the Apateu app. On that same screen there is a separate box, **"Text me Apateu alerts (optional)"**. The box is **unchecked by default**. Users who leave it unchecked still use the app normally; they get only the one-time code they asked for.

The words next to the box:

> **Text me Apateu alerts (optional)**  
> When someone likes your listing, accepts your request or messages you. Up to 5 texts a day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. [Terms](/termsOfuse/) · [Privacy](/privacypolicy/)

Users can also turn alerts on or off later in the app: Profile › Settings › Verification › Activity.

## What we send

Account alerts only. No marketing or promotional texts.

- Apateu: Amy liked your listing "Private room near USC" and offered $1,150. Open the app to reply. Reply STOP to opt out.
- Apateu: Amy accepted your request for "Private room near USC". Open the app to start chatting. Reply STOP to opt out.
- Apateu: Amy sent you a message. Open the app to read it. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Frequency, cost and help

- Up to 5 alerts a day; it depends on activity on your listings. No texts between 9 pm and 9 am Pacific.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply **STOP** to stop all texts, **START** to turn them back on, **HELP** for help.
- US mobile numbers only. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- We never sell or share your mobile number or text consent with anyone for their marketing. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacypolicy/) (section 2.2) and [Terms](/termsOfuse/) (section 5A).
